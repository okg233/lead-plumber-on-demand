Comprehensive Plumbing Services Hamilton

Emergency Plumbing — 24/7

Plumbing emergencies don't wait for convenient times. Our emergency team responds quickly to prevent water damage and restore your plumbing system. Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

🔧

Clogged Drains & Sinks

Professional kitchen and bathroom drain cleaning using hydro-jetting, snaking, and video inspection to identify and remove clogs safely.

🛀

Clogged Toilets

Professional toilet unclogging using specialized augers and drain snakes to safely remove blockages without damaging your plumbing. Fast same-day response available.

💧

Leaking Pipes

Hidden pipe leaks can cause serious water damage. We use advanced leak detection technology including thermal imaging and acoustic sensors to locate leaks and repair them.

⚙️

Faucet Installation & Repair

Expert faucet installation ensuring leak-free connections and optimal water flow. We install all faucet types including touchless models, pull-down kitchen faucets, and modern bathroom fixtures.

📷

Drain Camera Inspection

When drain problems persist, camera inspection provides clear visual evidence of blockages, cracks, root intrusion, and pipe damage without guesswork.

⚡

Water Heater Services

Water heater installation, repair, and replacement. We handle all types including tank and tankless models, ensuring proper sizing, efficient operation, and code-compliant installation.

🏝️

Burst & Frozen Pipes

Emergency response for frozen pipes and burst pipe emergencies. We respond quickly to thaw frozen pipes safely and repair burst pipes before extensive water damage occurs.

🕗

Weekend & After-Hours Service

Plumbing problems don't always happen during business hours. Our team is available on weekends and after hours — consistent quality regardless of the day or time.

🔄

Toilet Replacement

Modern, water-efficient toilet replacement — from removal to installation, ensuring your new toilet is properly sealed, leveled, and connected.

👮️

Sewage Drain Repair

Damaged or collapsed drain pipes repaired with lasting solutions. Camera inspection first to locate the problem accurately, then partial or full sewer line repair as needed.

🏠

Appliance Installation

Safe and proper installation for dishwashers, washing machines, refrigerators, and ice makers — water lines, drainage, and connections according to manufacturer specifications.
